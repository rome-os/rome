# Briefing

Your day, summarized twice — a morning and an evening brief that pull together
what actually matters.

## What it does

Briefing delivers two scheduled briefs a day, woven from your calendar, recent
messages, and the findings of scouting tasks the assistant runs for you in the
background. Each brief lands in the channel you choose, so you can skip the
constant notifications and get a calm rundown instead.

## Features

- **Scouting tasks** — write a detailed prompt and pick how often it runs (every
  15 minutes up to daily). The assistant carries it out on schedule and saves the
  findings for your next brief. Great for "watch Hacker News for posts about X",
  "track this topic", or "check for replies I owe people."
- **Morning & evening briefs** — fully configurable times (default 8:00 AM and
  11:00 PM). Each brief checks your calendar, recent messages, and every scouting
  finding gathered since the last brief.
- **Try with Rome** — based on how you use Rome and what you care about, the
  brief may suggest one or two things Rome could do for you: build or improve
  a website for your business, automate a task you keep doing by hand, or make
  a game or tracker for a hobby. It stays out when nothing clearly fits. Your
  chats and Rome Cloud profile are read only for briefs sent to you, never for
  a brief sent to someone else.
- **Delivered where you want** — send briefs to WhatsApp or email; pick the
  channel and recipient in settings.
- **Test anytime** — run a brief on demand to preview exactly what you'd receive.
- **Missing sources are skipped** — if a source isn't connected, the brief
  still arrives without it.

## When to use it

When you want a once-or-twice-a-day digest instead of a noisy feed, when you're
keeping an eye on a few topics and want them checked automatically, or when you'd
like a quick rundown of your calendar and messages each morning and night. You can
add scouting tasks in the app, or just ask in chat — "scout Hacker News for posts
about Rome every 6 hours."
