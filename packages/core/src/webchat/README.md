# Webchat

The dashboard chat, Rome's own chat and not a connection: its chats and projects, who owns each and who may open it, uploads, and continuing a turn after a restart or a usage limit.

## Imports

- Webchat may import Access, Agents, Apps, Chats and Participation, as [`lint/rules/contexts.ts`](../../../../lint/rules/contexts.ts) checks.
- It is imported by App host and Concierge.
