# Continued after the guardian signed in (2026-09-24 20:00 PDT)

Client pid 867 was signed in: its main window showed the chat list, and the store
keys were ready. Traces are in this folder, with the contact's name redacted.

- **Q1: AT-SPI can open the chat and send, with no click and no xdotool** (trace-s2,
  trace-s3-send1, trace-s3-send2).
  - The keys are Down, Up and Return, sent through `GenerateKeyboardEvent`. Down then
    Up makes the tree mark the current result `focused`, so the row is confirmed
    before Return.
  - The narrow window's Back button is reached through its SetFocus action plus a
    registry Space.
  - The registry's keys use XTest internally and follow X focus.
- **Q2: result rows can't be told apart by structure alone, but a text rule works**
  (trace-survey1).
  - Every row has the same role, the same parent (one flat list), the same states and
    interfaces, no attributes, no children, and no Selection interface.
  - The rule used instead: the row at index 1 carries exactly the target's name, is
    the row the tree marks `focused` after Down and Up, and follows a header that
    isn't `Internet search results` or `More` (a language table).
  - A list counts as current only once it holds a row named exactly after the query.
  - The same rule held at 480x440, 760x560, 878x640 and 1280x770.
- **Q3: AT-SPI state alone can't guard keys, and AT-SPI can't raise a minimized
  window.**
  - AT-SPI trails X input focus by 38 to 107 ms (median 84, 30 trials; trace-lag2).
  - Deterministic steal test (trace-race2): with the AT-SPI-only guard, 10 of 10 keys
    were sent and 10 of 10 landed in the thief. With the X check (XGetInputFocus),
    0 were sent.
  - Registry Alt+Tab (openbox) raised WeChat from behind Chrome 3 of 3 times, and from
    minimized 0 of 2. The pure-AT-SPI raise on minimized stopped safely 4 of 4
    (trace-rel-pure-minimized).
- **Scenario 4, with the X check** (trace-rel-final2): 50 of 50 dry runs reached the
  target, with 0 leaked bytes in a raw-mode thief xterm and 7 retries after focus
  steals. The earlier passes were rel-hybrid (32 of 35, before the retry) and
  rel-final (49 of 50, before the current-list rule).
- **Real sends:** 2, both confirmed in the store: `filehelper:4` and `filehelper:5`.
