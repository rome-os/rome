# Chats

Who said what, where, and as whom: chats, their messages and history, the accounts in them, and the connected accounts messages arrive on. The words it uses are defined in [`docs/domains/chats.md`](../../../../docs/domains/chats.md).

## Imports

- Chats may import Access, Agents, Apps, Connections, Participation and People, as [`lint/rules/contexts.ts`](../../../../lint/rules/contexts.ts) checks.
- It is imported by App host, Concierge, Connections, People and Webchat.
